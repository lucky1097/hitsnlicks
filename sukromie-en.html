Privacy Policy

Hits & Licks · beta · effective from 24. 11. 2026

This is a translation. Only the Slovak version is legally binding: Slovenčina

This explains what data the game Hits & Licks processes about you, why, for how long, and what rights you have. We write it plainly, but it applies in full under the GDPR.

1. Who Processes the Data

Game operator: vollgas s.r.o., Jozefa Kozáčeka 829/2, 960 01 Zvolen, IČO (company ID): 57 589 640
Contact for everything about your data: hitsnlickssupport@gmail.com

2. What Data

  • Account: email address and password. The password is stored only as an encrypted hash; nobody can see it.
  • Game: nickname, character, level, items, game money, missions, gang, arena points and other progress. Other players can see your nickname, level, class, gang and points (leaderboards, profiles, gangs).
  • Purchases: when you buy gold bars, Stripe processes the payment. For that it collects your email, country (for VAT), the details of your card or other payment method and technical data to prevent fraud. In our Stripe account we see what you bought, when, for how much, your email and your country; we do not see the full card number.
  • Bug reports: the text you send via “Report a bug”, plus the game version, screen, display size, browser and device type, and the game's most recent error messages. The game also logs technical errors by itself (without your text).
  • Technical data: with every connection, our providers (see point 4) see your IP address and browser type. They keep it briefly in their logs for operation and security.
  • Checking that you are not a robot: when you sign in, register and request a new password, the Cloudflare Turnstile service performs this check. For that it processes your IP address and technical data about your browser and device. It is not used for advertising.
  • Your browser: your sign-in and sound settings are stored in the browser's storage. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, we do not track you on other sites, and we do not sell data to anyone.

3. Why and on What Basis

  • Running the game and your account, including emails about the account (registration confirmation, new password): performance of a contract, i.e. the terms of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Purchases and accounting: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and a legal obligation under accounting and VAT law (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Security, protection against fraud, robots and abuse, bug fixing: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not send advertising emails. We do not make any automated decisions or profiling that would have legal effects on you.

4. Who Else Processes the Data

  • Supabase Inc.: database, sign-in, game server and account emails. The data is stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • Cloudflare Inc.: hosting of the game website, protection against attacks and checking that you are not a robot (Turnstile).
  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland): payment processing, VAT calculation and fraud prevention. For fraud prevention and its own legal obligations, Stripe is a separate controller under its own policy (stripe.com/privacy).
  • GitHub Inc.: storage of the game code. The game's old address on GitHub only redirects you here.

Supabase, Cloudflare and GitHub are companies based in the USA; Stripe belongs to a group based in the USA. If data leaves the EU, it is protected by the EU standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

5. How Long

  • Account, game and bug report data: as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete it right away.
  • Purchase records: 10 years under accounting law, even if you delete your account.
  • Database backups: up to 30 days; after that, deleted data is also gone from the backups.
  • Provider logs (for example IP address): according to their rules, usually a few days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected, erased, restricted, transferred to another controller, and the right to object to processing based on a legitimate interest.

  • Deleting your account you do yourself in the game: Settings → Account → Delete account. Your character, email and password are deleted.
  • For anything else, write to hitsnlickssupport@gmail.com. We reply within one month.
  • If you believe we handle your data wrongly, you can lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR), Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava, dataprotection.gov.sk.

7. Age

The game is intended only for persons aged 18 or over. If we find that an account belongs to a younger player, we will delete it.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will announce it in the game. See also the Terms of Use.
