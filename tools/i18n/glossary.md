# Hits & Licks translation glossary (SK → CZ / DE / EN)

Slovak is the source. Tone: street, gritty, short. The game says "ty" to the player: Czech tykání, German "du",
casual English. Keep `{placeholders}`, HTML tags, `&amp;`, numbers, `$` and `%` exactly as in the Slovak text.
Buttons and labels stay short (German especially: shorten rather than wrap).

## Names stay as they are

Don, Díler (the mission giver's name: never "Dealer"), Wexler, Kladivo, Ripper, every boss name
(Pitbull, Starý Moric, Hasák, …), player names, gang tags (CRV, HAD, …). Mission rivals' nicknames (Semienko, Klobúk, …)
are translated the way their mission stories say them.

## Street and place names

- Mesto 1 streets (Grabenská, Ladská, Kronprinzova, Kostolná, Východná, Bilkerská, Alžbetina, Západná, …): keep the
  name in its base form, change only the grammar around it: "Corner na Grabenskej" → CZ "Corner na Grabenské",
  DE "Corner in der Grabenská", EN "Corner on Grabenská".
- Mesto 2 is Prague's Old Town: in every language use the Czech street names: Valentinská, Žatecká, Karlova, Kozí,
  Melantrichova, Celetná, Dušní, Haštalská, Kaprova, Revoluční, 17. listopadu, Pařížská, Maiselova, Široká, Dlouhá.
  DE: "Corner in der Karlova", "Garage in der Revoluční"; EN: "Corner on Karlova", "Garage on Revoluční".

## Form

- Plural keys look like `misia|misie|misií` (Slovak: 1 | 2–4 | 5 and more). Czech gives 3 forms the same way,
  German and English give 2 (`Mission|Missionen`, `mission|missions`).
- Keys with `{name}` are sentences with gaps; the gap can move to wherever the language needs it.
- Keep the Slovak sentence's meaning, not its word order. Don't add or drop information.
- Quotation marks: Czech and German keep „ “; English uses “ ”.
